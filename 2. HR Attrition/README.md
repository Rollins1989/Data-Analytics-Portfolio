# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Overview

Analysis of employee workforce data to identify attrition patterns, data-quality issues, and characteristics associated with higher employee turnover.

## Business Questions

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which employee characteristics are associated with attrition?
- What does a higher-risk employee profile look like?
- Which workforce factors show the strongest relationships with attrition?

## Key Metrics

- **5,000 employees** analyzed
- **21.52% overall attrition**
- **647 employees** identified in the high-risk profile
- High-risk profile attrition rate: **30.8%**
- Job satisfaction showed a negative relationship with attrition in the analysis
